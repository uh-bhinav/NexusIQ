# EU/EEA Data Residency Requirements (DR-11)

**Effective:** 2024-03-01
**Owner:** Compliance

## Section 1: Scope

This policy applies to any vendor or subprocessor that stores, processes, or
transmits personal data belonging to a data subject located in the European
Union or European Economic Area, regardless of where the vendor itself is
headquartered.

## Section 2: Residency Requirement

In-scope data must be processed and stored within the EU/EEA. A transfer
outside the EU/EEA requires one of:

1. An adequacy decision from the European Commission covering the
   destination country, or
2. Standard Contractual Clauses executed between NexusIQ and the vendor,
   with a documented Transfer Impact Assessment, or
3. Binding Corporate Rules approved by a competent supervisory authority.

A vendor's SOC 2 report alone does not satisfy this requirement — SOC 2
attests to control effectiveness, not to the lawfulness of a specific
cross-border transfer mechanism.

## Section 3: Vendor Disclosure Obligation

Every vendor handling in-scope data must disclose, in writing, the specific
country or countries in which that data will be stored and processed. A
vendor submission that is silent on processing location cannot be assessed
against this policy and must be treated as unresolved, not as compliant by
default.

## Section 4: Subprocessors

Every subprocessor with access to in-scope data is subject to this same
residency requirement, and must be disclosed by the primary vendor.
