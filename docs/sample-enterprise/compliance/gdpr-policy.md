# GDPR Compliance Policy

**Effective:** 2023-05-25
**Owner:** Compliance

## Section 1: Lawful Basis

Any processing of personal data by a vendor on NexusIQ's behalf must be
covered by a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) that specifies the
lawful basis for processing under GDPR Article 6.

## Section 2: Breach Notification

Vendors must notify NexusIQ of a personal data breach without undue delay,
and in any event within 72 hours of the vendor becoming aware of it, so that
NexusIQ can meet its own regulator-notification obligation under GDPR
Article 33.

## Section 3: Data Subject Rights

Vendors must provide a documented process for fulfilling data subject
access, rectification, erasure, and portability requests within the
statutory one-month window (extendable to three months for complex
requests, per Article 12(3)).

## Section 4: Subprocessor Transparency

Vendors must maintain a current, publicly available list of subprocessors
with access to personal data, and must provide at least 30 days' advance
notice before adding a new subprocessor.

## Section 5: International Transfers

See DR-11 (EU/EEA Data Residency Requirements) for the specific residency
and transfer-mechanism requirements that implement GDPR Chapter V for
NexusIQ's vendor relationships.
