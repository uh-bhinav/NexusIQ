# Vendor Approval Policy (PR-07)

**Effective:** 2023-09-01
**Owner:** Procurement

## Section 1: Pre-Approval Requirements

Before any vendor may be approved for production use, Procurement must
confirm a signed Data Processing Addendum is on file and that the vendor's
most recent security assessment score is no more than 12 months old.

### 1.1 Required Documentation

The vendor must supply a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent, a list
of all subprocessors, and a completed data residency questionnaire covering
every jurisdiction in which the vendor may process NexusIQ data.

## Section 2: Approval Authority

### 2.1 Standard Approval

Vendors scoring Tier 2 or Tier 3 on the risk assessment may be approved by
the Procurement Lead without further escalation.

### 2.2 Elevated Approval

Tier 1 vendors, or any vendor requesting access to regulated data
categories (including any personal data subject to GDPR), require sign-off
from the Vendor Risk Committee in addition to Procurement.

## Section 3: Ongoing Obligations

Approved vendors are subject to an annual reassessment. Failure to complete
the reassessment within 30 days of the due date suspends the vendor's
approval pending review.
